WikiText-2 (raw) — data attribution
===================================

The file `wiki.test.raw` in this directory is the test split of the
WikiText-2 raw dataset.

Source:
    Salesforce / wikitext
    https://huggingface.co/datasets/Salesforce/wikitext
    configuration: wikitext-2-raw-v1
    download: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Salesforce/wikitext/resolve/main/wikitext-2-raw-v1/wikitext-2-raw.zip

License:
    Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported (CC BY-SA 3.0)
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

Reference:
    Stephen Merity, Caiming Xiong, James Bradbury, Richard Socher.
    "Pointer Sentinel Mixture Models." arXiv:1609.07843, 2016.

The dataset is derived from English Wikipedia. If you redistribute this file,
keep this notice and the CC BY-SA 3.0 terms with it.

Only `wiki.test.raw` is committed because it is the corpus used by
`llama-perplexity` (see `arena/config.py`). The train/valid splits are not used
by Local Arena and are intentionally ignored by `.gitignore`.
